This page is written for team captains. A captain can manage squads, scores, and match fees for their own team's fixtures — not for other teams, and not league/roster setup itself. For everything else (or an admin's view of the app), see the full Help page.

1. Getting access

Captain accounts are set up by an admin, and sign in the same way as everyone else.

  1. Ask an admin to add you at Admin → User Access, with the Captain access level and your player profile linked. This is also what marks you as captain of your team.
  2. Go to /login, enter your email, and click Send sign-in link.
  3. Open the email and click the link — you're in. No password to remember.

Only one person can be signed in to the main site at a time. If someone else is logged in, you'll see how long is left on their session. This doesn't affect the separate Match Day portal (section 5) — any number of captains can be in that at once, independently of the main site.

2. Setting up your team

Once signed in, open your team from the Teams page.

What you can change captain or admin

  • Team name and match fees — click Edit on your team's page. Toggling match fees on adds a fee-tracking section to every fixture (see section 4).

What an admin still handles

  • Adding or removing players from your roster
  • Registering a brand-new player who's never played for the club before
  • Assigning or changing who the captain is

Your actual "team setup," day to day, is really squad selection per fixture — that's section 3.

3. Picking a squad

A squad is who you're planning to field for a fixture, reserved ahead of time so eligibility issues surface early — not the final rubber-by-rubber pairing.

  1. Open the fixture — from Teams → Weekly Allocation (pick your league to see the whole week at once), or from the fixture's own page.
  2. Tick the players you want in the Squad section.
  3. Click Save Squad. If a selection would tie a player to your team for the season, you'll see a confirmation before it saves.

Reading eligibility

Each player in the squad picker shows a status dot:

Green — already tied to your team this season. All good.

Amber — fine to pick, but selecting them will tie them to your team going forward, locking them out of any lower-ranked team.

Red — ineligible. They're already tied to a different team at the same level or below.

A player's fixtures for your team also count toward every team ranked below yours. Once their combined total across your team and lower ones hits the league's threshold, they're locked to the lowest team they've played for — they can still move up, never down.

4. Entering results

You can enter results directly on a fixture page any time — the Match Day portal in section 5 is simply the courtside shortcut for the same job.

  1. Open the fixture and select the players for each rubber from the dropdowns — score boxes unlock once both are chosen.
  2. Fill in the set scores. Use T to mark a tie, or W to cycle through a walkover (your side → opponent → cleared).
  3. Click Save Results once every rubber is complete — it only activates when the grid is full. The fixture's overall score updates automatically.

Match fees: if you've turned on match fees for your team (section 2), a Fees section appears on the fixture page — tick players off as they pay and click Save Fees.

5. The Match Day portal

A separate, phone-friendly login for filling in a scoresheet courtside — a memory aid alongside your paper scorecard. Nothing you enter here touches the real fixture until you review and confirm it afterwards.

Logging in courtside

  1. On the sign-in page, follow "Captain at a match?" — or go straight to /pitchside/login.
  2. Enter your email and the last 4 digits of your LTA number. No password, no sign-in link — you're straight in.
  3. Pick the fixture you're at (or preparing for) from the list. It shows today plus the next several days, so you can enter your pairs in advance and add scores once they're actually played.

Filling in the scoresheet

It's a two-step wizard: pairs, then scores.

  1. Pairs — type in your pairs (suggestions are limited to your own team's roster) and the opponent's pairs (typed freely, no suggestions). Tap Continue to Scores.
  2. Scores — fill in whichever rubbers have finished, in any order, as results come in. Tap W/O on a rubber to mark a walkover instead — tap again to switch sides, again to clear.

Save Progress is safe to use anytime — nothing's final yet. Submit Final marks the scoresheet ready for review.

Confirming afterwards captain or admin

Once submitted, a banner appears on the fixture's real page for you (or an admin) to review. It opens the normal results form, pre-filled from what you entered courtside — check it over, fix anything, and save to write it into the fixture for real.

If a name you typed doesn't match any existing player, confirming is blocked — you'll need to ask an admin to register them first, then come back and save. This only applies to your own players; opponent names are never registered, just kept for reference on the confirm screen.
